# PROCOGNEX

Welcome to the **PROCOGNEX** GitHub profile.

We are a consulting and engineering company focused on **Process Intelligence, Process Mining, Task Mining, Data Science, and Process Improvement**.

We help organizations understand how their processes really work, connect operational data with business context, and turn insights into measurable improvements.

## Vision

To make organizations more intelligent by connecting **processes, data, technology, and people**.

We believe process intelligence should go beyond dashboards and analysis. It should help companies understand what is happening, why it is happening, and what should happen next.

## Mission

Our mission is to help organizations **turn process insights into real business improvement**.

We combine business understanding with technical execution to identify opportunities, uncover inefficiencies, and design solutions that improve how organizations operate.

Our work typically combines:

- **Process Mining**
- **Task Mining**
- **Data Science & Analytics**
- **Process Intelligence**
- **Data Integration**
- **Process Automation & Orchestration**
- **Business Context & Operational Analysis**
- **Change Management**

We do not stop at identifying opportunities.

Our approach connects **analysis, engineering, implementation, and organizational change** to help improvements move from insight into production.

## How We Work

Our projects typically follow a simple principle:

**Understand → Analyze → Improve → Implement → Measure**

We start by understanding the business context and the processes behind the data.

From there, we combine process intelligence and data analysis to identify bottlenecks, inefficiencies, automation opportunities, and areas for improvement.

When required, our engineering capabilities help turn those findings into scalable technical solutions.
